## Before you send

- **Do not paste the report into the email body.** Outlook renders mail with Word, which drops flex, grid, custom properties and backdrop-filter, and Gmail strips the `<head>`. The page would arrive broken.
- **Send the link, and attach a PDF** if some recipients cannot reach the SharePoint library. Print to PDF from the browser with "Background graphics" switched on; the report opens its own detail tables for the export.
- The figures above are the July view. If you send the quarter or the contract-to-date view instead, the headline numbers change: H1 2026 net is SAR 2,305,782 and the contract to date is SAR 3,128,009.
