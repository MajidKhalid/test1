FinOps Builder · GCP monthly report generator

Internal operator tool for the FinOps owner only. Never publish or share this file. The file it generates (FinOps_Dashboard.html) is the shareable one. Nothing you drop here leaves your browser.
  1. Run the monthly pull per GCP_Data_Pull_Runbook.md (Reports CSV by Service, for the month and for contract-to-date; sandbox-filtered versions of the same; quarter range only in quarter-end months).
  2. Drop each CSV into its slot below, check the labels and notes, then Generate.
  3. Upload the downloaded FinOps_Dashboard.html to SharePoint, overwriting the existing file. The link stays the same; viewers see the new edition.

The automatic highlight picks the fastest-growing service in the period (or the concentration fact when no growth figure exists). Type your own sentence to override it.

The published page carries an EN / AR switch. Leave a field empty and the Arabic view falls back to the English text. Page furniture, section titles and the figure labels are already translated.
