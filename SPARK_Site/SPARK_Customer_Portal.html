01 · What SPARK is

The Framework, implemented — not a separate initiative

The Ministry signed its AI Governance Framework in July 2026. SPARK is that framework converted from signed text into a running system — and the sandbox it governs is open to every innovator, with or without AI: every workspace is created inside the controls, and every AI use case lands in the AI Register automatically.

The SPARK boundary: your workspace calls the model engine over the internal network only, and the AI Register is updated automatically.

A GCP playground — for any idea

An isolated GCP playground environment per use case — the compute, storage, and services your build needs, for every innovator, with or without AI.

Approved AI — as a service

Language models approved to Ministry requirements, on Ministry GPUs behind one internal address — no procurement, no setup, no waiting.

02 · Who SPARK is for

Launching in three phases

SPARK opens in phases, so the service matures with each audience before it widens to the next.

Phase 1 — now

IT & digital staff

ITDT teams build the first use cases and prove the service from the inside.

Phase 2

All Ministry of Energy staff

Every Ministry employee can apply for a workspace — or simply adopt published tools without building anything.

Phase 3

The energy ecosystem

Innovators across the KSA energy ecosystem build inside the sandbox through a Ministry sponsor.
