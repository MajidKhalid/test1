Base pattern: Tunis 2 nights → Hammamet 2 → Sousse 1 → Tunis 2.

New route philosophy

We are not sleeping in one place.

The plan now uses three bases. You get the deep-Medina experience in Tunis, a genuine stay in Hammamet, then one night inside/next to Sousse’s historic core before returning to Tunis for the final two nights and the flight home.

Mother-friendly rule

Move cities, not luggage every morning.

We change hotels only three times. Private driver days carry the luggage, while the most uneven sites have shorter walking windows and café breaks.
