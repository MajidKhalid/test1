Core recommendation

Stay inside the Medina for all 7 nights.

Do not split the trip across hotels. Your stay should be one of the experiences. First choice: Dar Ben Gacem. If you want more space and practicality for your mother: Dar Ben Turkia.

Comfort rule

Ask about stairs before paying.

Historic Tunis means uneven paving and limited car access. Get room floor, stair count and taxi drop-off distance in writing.

If comfort becomes the priority: Dar El Jeld jumps to #2 because its large suites, spa and service are easier while still keeping you in the Medina.
